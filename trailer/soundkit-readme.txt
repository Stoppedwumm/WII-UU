WII-UU Sound Kit
================

Stingers and sound effects for WII-UU trailers and videos. Every sound was synthesized by
WII-UU's own code (trailer/SoundKit.java), so there are no samples and nothing to license:
use them freely in your WII-UU videos.

All files are 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo WAV, peaking at -1 dBFS. Musical sounds are in F major,
the key of WII-UU's menu theme, so they sit well under or after its music.

Stingers
--------
Boot Chime    WII-UU's real start-up chime: rising bells over a warm pad.
Logo Impact   A boom and crash with the chime on top and a shimmering pad. For the logo reveal.
Menu Jazz     The menu's sound: electric piano, vibraphone and soft drums, the theme's opening.
8-bit         A quick chiptune run up into a chord.
House Drop    One bar of riser and snare roll (128 BPM) into a drop hit. The drop lands at 1.875 s.
Outro         A gentle ending: vibraphone falling over a long chord, with the chime.
Swoosh Bell   A short whoosh that lands on a bell, for title cards.

SFX
---
Whoosh Short / Long / Down   Air moving across the picture (Long pans right to left).
Riser 4s / 8s                Noise and a climbing synth, building to a cut.
Downlifter                   The opposite: falling after a drop or a cut.
Reverse Swell                A reversed cymbal that peaks right at its end; put the end on the cut.
Impact Boom / Hit            A big cinematic boom, and a short punchy hit.
Sub Drop                     A deep falling bass hit.
Shimmer                      Bells glittering upward, for sparkles and reveals.
Glitch Stutter               A chord chopped into faster and faster repeats.
Tape Stop                    A chord slowing to a halt.
Pop                          A small bubbly pop, for text or icons popping in.
Snare Roll 2 Bars            A build-up roll at 128 BPM.

UI
--
UI Move / Select / Back / Bump / Chime   WII-UU's real menu sounds.
UI Phone Paired                          Two bells: a phone has joined as the GamePad.
UI Notification                          A soft two-note alert.
