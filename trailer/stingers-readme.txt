WII-UU Visual Stingers
======================

Animated logo stings and transitions for WII-UU trailers and videos, 1920x1080 at 30 fps, each
with its sound from the WII-UU Sound Kit already in place. Made by WII-UU's own code
(trailer/VisualStingers.java): use them freely in your WII-UU videos.

Every stinger comes twice:
  .mov            ProRes 4444 with a transparent background and 48 kHz audio. Put it on a
                  track above your footage (Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, After Effects).
  (preview).mp4   The same on WII-UU's dark background, for a quick look or direct use.

Stingers
--------
01 Logo Spin-In   3.0 s  The 3D logo spins in, lands at 0.9 s with a shockwave and sparks.
02 Logo Reveal    3.5 s  The 3D logo glows into view face on, with a light sweep (boot chime).
03 House Drop     4.0 s  Streaks and a spectrum ring build up; the logo slams in on the drop
                         at 1.875 s (128 BPM).
04 Glitch Logo    1.5 s  RGB split and torn slices resolve into the logo, then glitch away.
05 8-bit Pixel    2.0 s  The logo builds up from big pixels in time with the chiptune run.
06 End Card       6.0 s  The logo, "Get WII-UU free" and wiiuu.stoppedwumm.net. Lay it over
                         your last shot, or use the preview on its own.

Transitions
-----------
07 Swoosh Wipe    1.2 s  A blue band with the logo sweeps across. Cut between your two clips at
                         0.6 s, where the band covers the whole screen.
08 Tile Pop       1.6 s  White Wii U menu tiles pop in until they cover the screen (0.5 to
                         0.95 s, the logo shows), then pop out. Cut at 0.7 s.
